Meet Tide — a customizable Codex & Work usage widget for KDE Plasma 6.

Aquarium, lava, motorsport, analytics, or a clean minimal display. Make it yours with transparent surfaces, custom fonts, and meter-only layouts.

Free and open source, created by Nicholas Hillsdale.
https://github.com/nchillsdale-source/tide-codex-kde-widget

Demo uses illustrative data. Shared Codex & Work allowance; ordinary ChatGPT conversations are excluded.
