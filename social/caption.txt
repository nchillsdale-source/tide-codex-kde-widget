Tide for KDE Plasma 6: five desktop displays, transparent surfaces, animated fish, molten lava, motorsport, and clean analytics.

Now with local Codex token totals and usage rates on every display, an organized settings panel, live style preview, custom fonts, and built-in help.

Open source. Created by Nicholas Hillsdale.
https://github.com/nchillsdale-source/tide-codex-kde-widget

Demo uses illustrative data. Chat conversations are excluded; local token counts are not account-wide billing totals.
